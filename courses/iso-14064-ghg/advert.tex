As organizations strengthen their climate strategies and respond to evolving
reporting requirements, understanding greenhouse-gas accounting has never been
more important. This practical, industry-focused programme builds a clear
understanding of carbon-accounting principles and their real-world

organizational and product carbon footprints, and understanding climate

the knowledge and tools to perform GHG accounting, develop carbon-footprint
assessments, prepare GHG reports and support climate-project implementation.}
