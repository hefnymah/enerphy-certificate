\coursesection{Course Overview}
A practical, industry-focused programme that builds a clear understanding of
carbon-accounting principles and their real-world application --- navigating
international GHG standards, distinguishing organizational and product carbon
footprints, and understanding climate projects for credible reporting.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.4cm}p{9cm}@{}}
  \textbf{Duration}   & 2 days / 16 hours \\[2pt]
  \textbf{Delivery}   & Online --- live, instructor-led \\[2pt]
  \textbf{Standards}  & ISO 14064, ISO 14067, GHG Protocol, CORSIA \\[2pt]
  \textbf{Assessment} & Final assessment on completion \\
\end{tabular}

\coursesection{Learning Objectives}
On completion of this course, participants will be able to:
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1pt}\setlength\parskip{0pt}
  \item Understand the climate agenda and regulatory landscape in the Middle East.
  \item Apply greenhouse-gas emissions assessment and reporting methodologies.
  \item Work with international GHG standards and verification approaches.
  \item Prepare corporate carbon-footprint reports aligned with ISO 14064.
  \item Assess product carbon footprints aligned with ISO 14067.
  \item Manage GHG removals, emissions reductions and climate projects.
\end{itemize}
