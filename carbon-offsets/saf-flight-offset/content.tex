\coursesection{Reduction Method — Sustainable Aviation Fuel}
The contribution funds certified SAF delivered into the aviation fuel supply,
displacing fossil jet fuel. Environmental attributes are tracked and retired on
the holder's behalf so the reduction is claimed only once.
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1pt}\setlength\parskip{0pt}
  \item Feedstock: \feedstock
  \item Lifecycle reduction vs.\ fossil jet fuel: \lifecycle
  \item Chain-of-custody: \custody
\end{itemize}

\coursesection{Methodology \& Standards}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1pt}\setlength\parskip{0pt}
  \item GHG Protocol Corporate Standard \& Scope~3 (Category~6) accounting.
  \item ICAO CORSIA eligible-fuels principles for SAF emissions reduction.
  \item Emission factors: DEFRA / ICAO published datasets.
\end{itemize}
